How to play

Defend the keep

Foes march along the glowing road from the portal to your keep. Each one that gets through costs lives, and bosses cost many. Survive every wave to win the map.

Build

Pick a tower on the right (or press 1–6), then click an open tile beside the road. Hold Shift to place several. Right-click or Esc cancels. On touch screens, tap a tile once to preview and again to build.

Upgrade

Click a tower to see its stats. Every tower has three levels, then a choice of two specialists. Selling returns 70% of what you spent, or all of it if no wave has started since you built it.

Waves

Once a wave has fully arrived, the next starts in 20 seconds. Call it early for bonus gold. Clearing a wave pays a bounty too.

Armor, wards and flyers

Armor blunts arrows and shells. Wards blunt magic. Fire ignores half of armor. Flyers can only be hit by Archer, Mage and Frost towers.

Spells

Starfall (Q) drops a star on an area. Arcane Snare (W) roots foes in place. Both recharge over time.
